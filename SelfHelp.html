Interview Anxiety Help

This page is here to help you feel calm and confident before your job interview. You will find short exercises, calming videos, and useful tips that can make a big difference.

Breathing Exercise

Relax with this 5-minute breathing session. Just follow along and breathe deeply.

Body Relaxation

Watch this short video to stretch and reduce body tension. Moving your body helps reduce stress.

Motivational Quotes

  • “You are capable of amazing things.”
  • “Every expert was once a beginner.”
  • “One step at a time.”
  • “Believe in yourself.”

Extra Tips

  • Sleep well before your interview
  • Prepare your answers in advance
  • Practice with a friend or in front of a mirror
  • Take a deep breath before you speak
