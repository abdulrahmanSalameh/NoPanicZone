Introductory Paragraph

We believe that performing well in an interview doesn’t only depend on your skills and experience but also on your mental preparation. This website aims to support you psychologically and emotionally before and during interviews by offering practical tips and reliable resources that help reduce stress and build self-confidence.

What is Interview Stress?

Interview stress is a natural response of the body when facing situations that require focus or challenge, such as job interviews. It can negatively affect your thinking and concentration, which may reflect on your overall performance. Understanding the causes of this stress helps in dealing with it in a healthy and effective way.

Interview Tips:

  • Prepare well and research the company.
  • Practice answers to common questions.
  • Take deep breaths before the interview.
  • Get a good night's sleep the day before.
  • Remember: It’s not a test, it’s about talking about yourself!
