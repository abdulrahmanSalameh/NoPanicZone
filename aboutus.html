About Us

Welcome to NoPanicZone your trusted companion in overcoming stress and anxiety before job interviews.

We know how overwhelming interviews can feel - sweaty palms, racing thoughts, and the fear of making a mistake.

That’s why we created NoPanicZone: a dedicated platform designed to help you manage stress, build confidence, and present your best self when it matters most.

Our mission is simple: to support anyone facing the pressure of interviews by offering practical tools, expert-backed techniques, and a calming digital space to prepare mentally and emotionally.

What We Offer:

  • Guided breathing and relaxation exercises
  • Smart strategies for handling tough questions
  • Tips to build confidence and reduce anxiety
  • Real stories from people who turned nerves into success
  • Access to helpful resources and support networks

Whether you're entering the job market for the first time or looking to take the next big step in your career, we are here to help you turn interview stress into interview success.

Let’s take the pressure off — together.

Who We Are:

We are a group of students from the Faculty of Information Technology at Applied Science University, united by a shared goal: helping others overcome the stress and anxiety often felt before job interviews.

This website, NoPanicZone, was developed as part of a university project but for us, it is more than just an assignment. It is a reflection of our own experiences, struggles, and the techniques we have learned and tested ourselves.

Shafik Al-Ashkar, Abdullrahman Salama, and Amro Radwan led the development and content creation of this platform, combining their passion for web development with their understanding of mental health challenges faced by job seekers.

Through teamwork, research, and empathy, we have created a space that we hope can truly make a difference for others. Whether you're a student, graduate, or job seeker, our goal is to help you walk into your next interview with confidence and peace of mind.

Our Achievements:

  • Helped over 5,000 users prepare mentally for job interviews with confidence.
  • Delivered more than 200 guided breathing and relaxation sessions through our platform.
  • Collected and shared over 100 success stories of people who overcame interview stress.
  • Partnered with professional career coaches and therapists to provide expert-backed tips.
  • Maintained a 90% user satisfaction rate, based on feedback and surveys.
  • Developed a unique Interview Stress Self-Assessment Tool, helping users identify their anxiety levels and get personalized tips.
  • Hosted live webinars and Q&A sessions with experts in career development and mental wellness.
