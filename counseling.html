Online Counseling Services

If you are feeling anxious or stressed about job interviews, online counseling can help you prepare mentally and emotionally. Here are some trusted platforms where you can speak to professionals.

BetterHelp

Talk to licensed therapists online to manage interview stress, build confidence, and improve communication skills.

How to book: Create an account, complete a short quiz, and get matched with a therapist. Sessions via video, chat, or phone.

Visit BetterHelp

Talkspace

Online therapy and coaching for job-related anxiety and personal development. Choose a plan that suits your schedule.

How to book: Sign up, pick a subscription, and start messaging your assigned therapist.

Visit Talkspace

7 Cups

Free emotional support from trained listeners and affordable therapy options for job seekers.

How to access: Talk instantly with trained listeners, or book professional sessions.

Visit 7 Cups

Local Support Groups

Connect with local or online support groups that focus on career stress, interview preparation, and confidence building.

  • Psychology Today Support Groups
  • Mental Health America Find Support

You are not alone — help is available, and you can overcome job interview stress with the right support.
