Understanding Interview Anxiety

Interview anxiety is real — and you're not alone. It can show up in many ways: a racing heartbeat, sweaty hands, dry mouth, or even forgetting what to say. It often comes from fear of judgment, low confidence, or lack of preparation. But the good news is: it’s manageable — and we’re here to help.
